The Concept

Given the task of creating an animatronic artifact with one articulateing part. To meet this criteria we decided on a pterodactyl with flapping wings as the articulateing section.

Shown above are the initial designs I came up with in order to move the limbs. I took inspiration from ligaments. The idea was a motor in the body would pull on some sort of string attached to the outer limb raising the limb. The designs are different ways to connect the string to the limbs.

The first image shows a design inspired by fishing rod eyes. This design was not used in the final as although strong when made from metal, fibreglass, carbon fibre we are using 3d printing so the design was not suitable.

The second design instead shows raised rollers. This design was a set on protruding rollers these would have greater strength than the eyes and would better survive the compressive force. However if we wished to add a “skin” layer other the model there protrusions would interfere with it and so were not suitable.

The third design was similar to the second except the rollers were made internally. This design fixed the previous issue of interfering with the skin. Overall this design was worth prototyping however in order to bring the wing back down after raising it was mirrored top and bottom as seen in image four.
